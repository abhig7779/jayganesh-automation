# Google review replies: house style

One style for every reply, whether a person or the cloud job (`src/lib/reply-writer.mjs`) writes it. Built from an audit of all 776 live replies on 30 Oct 2026 and the owner's feedback.

## Who we are
Jay Ganesh Car Accessories & Detailing Studio, Kawadipat Toll Naka, Loni Kalbhor, Pune. Spelling is **Jay Ganesh**, never "Jai". (10 old replies say "Jai Ganesh Car Accessories Team"; don't copy that.)

## Rules
1. **Read the whole review, then answer only what this reviewer wrote.** Use their first name, and name the car or work they mention (Grand Vitara, seat covers, PPF…). Never invent details. Skip the name if it is a placeholder or a business ("Kidoo cartoon TV", "Krushikranti Agro Agency").
2. **Language and script follow the review.** English → English. Hindi or Marathi written in English letters → reply in that language in English letters. Devanagari (or "(Translated by Google)" from Marathi/Hindi) → Devanagari. No text, rating only → simple English.
3. **Respectful address:** "<First name> ji" (sometimes "sir" or "madam") in Marathi, Hindi or mixed replies; in English use the first name, with "ji" or "sir" only now and then. Vary it.
4. **Short and warm:** 1–3 sentences, like a shop owner who knows his customers.
5. **Vary the opening.** Don't start with "Thank you" every time: in the last audit 62 of 139 replies did. Mix with "Glad…", "Happy to hear…", "Great to see…", "Pleasure serving you…". Aim for under 1 in 4 starting "Thank you".
6. **No numbers:** no prices, discounts, offers, links or hashtags. You may echo the reviewer's own words ("glad you liked the rates") but never quote or confirm a figure, and never confirm a discount.
7. **Never ask a reviewer to change, raise or remove a rating.** Never argue or blame the customer.
8. **Emoji:** at most one, and only on positive replies (🙏 or 🚗). None on complaints.
9. **Sign-off:** English replies end `– Team Jay Ganesh`; Marathi or Hindi in Devanagari end `– टीम जय गणेश`.
10. **1–3★ or a complaint:** apologise for the specific problem, say the owner wants to speak personally, give the number **99813 97676** (call or WhatsApp). No promises of refunds or free work. These replies are not auto-posted; the owner approves them in a GitHub issue.
11. **Odd cases:**
    - A 1★ with a clearly positive text is probably a mis-tap: thank them for the kind words, no apology.
    - A reviewer called "Jayganesh Car" or any shop-owned name: don't reply; report or remove the review.
    - Report suspected fake reviews only if the reviewer's history shows it; never report genuine ones.

## Examples that match this style
- English: *Avinash ji, thanks a lot! That Blaupunkt Android player with tweeters is a great upgrade for the Tiago. Enjoy the sound, and thanks for recommending us too. – Team Jay Ganesh*
- Rating only: *Thank you for the 5-star rating, Pratik! We really appreciate it. – Team Jay Ganesh*
- Complaint: *Husen ji, we're truly sorry … Our owner would like to speak with you personally. Please call or WhatsApp him on 99813 97676. – Team Jay Ganesh*
- Hindi in English letters: *S G ji, aapko jo pareshani hui uske liye hume sach mein afsos hai … Kripya owner se seedha 99813 97676 par call ya WhatsApp kariye.*
