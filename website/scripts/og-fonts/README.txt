Fonts for the share cards (scripts/og-cards.mjs). Both are free for commercial
use under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org), from
https://github.com/google/fonts:
- ArchivoBlack-Regular.ttf  (Archivo Black, the site's display face at weight 900)
- Inter-SemiBold.ttf        (Inter 600, the small text on the cards)
They are only read at build time and are not served with the site.
