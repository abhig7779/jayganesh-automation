# Jay Ganesh CRM: Staff guide

This guide is for the counter, the workshop and the finance desk. Each job has three parts: **When** you do it, **Steps**, and **If something goes wrong**.

**Three rules that make everything easier**
1. The customer's **mobile number** finds everything. Type 10 digits and the app finds the customer.
2. You only see the buttons your role can use. If you can't see a button, ask the Owner.
3. Nothing is lost if the internet drops. The bill stays on your screen and saves when the net is back.

**Keys on the counter PC**

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| F2 | New bill |
| `/` (or F4) | Jump to the item search box |
| F3 | Jump to the customer mobile box |
| ↑ ↓ then Enter | Pick an item from the list |
| `+` and `−` | More or less quantity on the highlighted line |
| Del | Remove the highlighted line |
| D | Add a discount |
| 1 2 3 4 5 | Pay mode: Cash, UPI, Card, Split, Later |
| Q | Show the UPI QR big |
| Ctrl+Enter | Save the bill |
| Esc | Go back |

## 1. Signing in

**When:** at the start of your shift.

**Steps**
1. Open the CRM web address on your PC or phone (the Owner will give it to you).
2. Type your **mobile number** (or email) in the first box.
3. Type your password. Tap **Show** if you want to see what you typed.
4. Click **Sign in**.
5. When you finish, use the menu to sign out, especially on the shared counter PC.

**If something goes wrong**
- *Wrong password:* try again once. Then ask the Owner or Admin to reset it (Staff, your name, New password).
- *You are signed out by itself:* the app signs you out after you sit idle for a while. Sign in again. Half-made bills are kept.
- *Too many tries:* wait a few minutes and try again.

## 2. Making a bill on the counter PC (POS)

**When:** a customer buys something at the counter.

**Steps**
1. Press **F2** (or click **New bill**). The cursor is in the **mobile** box.
2. Type the customer's 10-digit mobile.
   - *Known customer:* the name, cars, last visit and any **Due** (red) show up. Click the car they came with, or **+ Add car**.
   - *New customer:* type the **Name**, pick the car model, and tap how they heard about us (Walk-in, Instagram, WhatsApp ad, Google, Friend). They are saved with the bill.
   - *No number:* click **Skip, walk-in without number**. No WhatsApp bill will go, so try to ask for the number.
3. Press **/** and start typing the item. Press **↑ ↓** and **Enter** to add it. The cursor stays in the box for the next item.
   - **`2*gleam`** adds 2 pieces of Gleam.
   - **Barcode:** scan it at any time. The item is added at once.
   - You can also click a tile under **Popular counter items** or **Recent on this PC**.
   - Item not found? Click **Add as custom item?**, type the name, price and GST, then **Add**.
4. Check the lines. Use `+` and `−` for quantity. Items with no stock show **Not in stock**, but you can still bill them.
5. *Discount (optional):* press **D**, type the amount (₹ or %), pick a reason (Festival offer, Regular customer, Bargain), click **Done**. Above the Owner's limit it needs approval.
6. Pick how they pay, with keys **1 to 5** or a click:
   - **Cash** (1): type "Cash given" and the app shows the change.
   - **UPI** (2): the QR shows with the amount filled in. Press **Q** to make it bigger. Type the UPI ref number if you want.
   - **Card** (3): type the slip number if you want.
   - **Split** (4): type how much is cash, UPI and card. "Remaining" must reach ₹0.
   - **Later** (5): type "Paid now" (can be 0). The rest becomes a **Due**. Sales staff can leave up to the limit the Owner set. Above that, an Admin or the Owner must do it.
7. Leave **Send bill on WhatsApp** ticked (it needs the 10-digit number).
8. Click the yellow **Save bill & send WhatsApp** button, or press **Ctrl+Enter**.
9. On the **Bill saved** screen:
   - **Print A4** or **Thermal 80 mm** prints the bill.
   - **Share PDF** shares the file.
   - **Send on WhatsApp** opens WhatsApp with the message and bill link (manual mode).
   - **New bill** (F2) starts the next one.

**If something goes wrong**
- *"Mobile number needs 10 digits":* fix the number. The Save button works only when it is right.
- *Amber bar "No internet":* keep going. The bill is saved on your PC and goes in when the net returns. Do not make the same bill twice.
- *Red "Not sent":* the number may not be on WhatsApp. Click **Send manually**. WhatsApp opens with the message. Press Send there.
- *Bill must be redone:* ask an Admin or the Owner to cancel it (section 12). Then make a new one.
- *You closed the page by mistake:* open **New bill** again. The draft comes back. Click **Discard** if you don't want it.
- *No QR shows:* the Owner has not put the UPI ID in Settings yet.

## 3. Making a bill on the phone

**When:** you are away from the counter PC.

**Steps**
1. Tap the big **+ New bill** button.
2. **Step 1, Customer:** type the mobile number. Pick the car, or fill the new customer's name and car.
3. **Step 2, Items:** search or tap a popular item. Use the **− 1 +** buttons. Swipe a row left to remove it. Tap **Next**.
4. **Step 3, Payment:** pick Cash, UPI, Card, Split or Later. Keep **Send bill on WhatsApp** on. Tap the yellow save button.

**If something goes wrong:** same as section 2. The **Back** arrow takes you to the previous step and nothing is lost.

---

## 4. Quotations and follow-ups (the most important daily list)

Every quotation and every price enquiry must be chased. The **Follow-ups** list tells you who to call today. **Open it first thing every morning.**

### 4a. Making a quotation

**When:** a customer asks for a price and says they will think about it.

**Steps**
1. Click **Quotations**, then **+ New quotation**.
2. Type the customer's **mobile number** (needed, or we can't follow up). Add the name and car if new.
3. Search and add the items, like on a bill.
4. Check **Valid for** (7 days is the usual) and add a note if you want.
5. Click **Save & send on WhatsApp**, or press **Ctrl+Enter**. WhatsApp opens with the quote PDF link. Press Send there.
6. The app plans the follow-ups by itself (Day 1, 3, 6 and 10).
7. If you don't want to send it right now, click **Save without sending**.

**Only a phone call, no formal quote?** On the Quotations page click **Asked price**. Type the mobile, what they asked for and the price you told them, then **Save & follow up**. It joins the same list.

### 4b. Working through today's follow-ups

**When:** every morning, and again in the afternoon.

**Steps**
1. Click **Follow-ups** in the menu. The number shows how many are due. **Red** rows are late.
2. The top row is selected. On the right you see the customer, the car, the items and the message that will be sent.
3. Click **WhatsApp** (or press **W**). WhatsApp opens with a ready message. You can change the text first. Press Send in WhatsApp.
4. Or click **Call** (or press **C**) to phone the customer.
5. Now click **Log result** (or press **L**) and pick what happened. Keys **1 to 7** pick the result:

   | Key | Result | What you do next |
   |---|---|---|
   | 1 | Coming on… | Pick the date they will visit |
   | 2 | Thinking / call later | Pick Tomorrow, +3 days or a date |
   | 3 | No answer | It comes back tomorrow |
   | 4 | Wants a better price | The Owner or Admin will see it |
   | 5 | Bought elsewhere | Closes it as lost. Add the price or shop in the note |
   | 6 | Not interested | Closes it as lost |
   | 7 | Wrong number | Closes it |

6. Type a short **Note** if useful, then click the save button or press **Enter**. The row moves away.
7. Use **↑ ↓** to move between rows and **/** to search by name, car number or quote.
8. Filters at the top show the groups (today, overdue and others).

**If the customer arrives and buys:** when the number gets a bill, the quote is marked **Won** by itself.

### 4c. Turning a quote into a bill

**When:** the customer comes in to buy.

**Steps**
1. On a new bill, type their mobile. A yellow **Open quote** line shows. Click **Use quote**.
2. Or open the quote (Follow-ups, **Open quote**, or Quotations list) and click **Convert to bill**.
3. The items are copied. Check prices, take payment and save as in section 2.

**If something goes wrong**
- *Customer asks for a different price:* open the quote and click **Revise**.
- *Quote must be closed:* open it and click **Mark as lost**, add the reason, then **Close as lost**.
- *Wrong staff member has it:* the Owner or Admin can click **Reassign** on the quote.
- *Nothing in the list:* it says "Nobody to chase". Good. Make sure every price you give is logged with a quote or **Asked price**.

## 5. Adding a customer and a car

**When:** a new customer, outside a bill. (A bill adds new customers by itself.)

**Steps**
1. Click **Customers**, then **+ Add customer**.
2. Type the **Mobile number**. If it already exists, the app says "This number already belongs to ..." and offers the profile. Open it instead.
3. Type the **Name**. Pick how they heard about us.
4. Add the car number (optional). Open **More details** for area, second mobile, email and notes.
5. Press **Enter** to save.
6. To add another car later, open the customer and click **+ Add car**.

**If something goes wrong**
- *Wrong spelling:* open the customer and click **Edit**.
- *Customer left or added by mistake:* click **Archive**. History is kept. Typing the number on a new bill brings them back.
- *Notes for the team:* write in **Notes for the team** and click **Save note**.

---

## 6. Checking stock and price

**When:** a customer asks "how much?" or "do you have it?".

**Steps**
1. Click **Stock** (or use **Check stock & price**).
2. Type 2 or 3 letters of the name, brand, code or what people call it (for example `glea`, `creta mat`, `ppf`).
3. Each result shows the price (big), the stock (green is fine, amber is low, red is zero) and the shelf if set.
4. Click a result to see the price, the normal price (MRP), warranty and compatible cars.
5. Click **Add to new bill** to start a bill with it, or **Send price on WhatsApp**.

**If something goes wrong**
- *Not found:* try fewer letters or the brand. Then ask the Owner to add the product.
- *Stock looks wrong:* tell the Owner or Admin. Only they can correct stock.

---

## 7. Collecting a due payment

**When:** a customer pays an old balance.

**Steps**
1. Click **Collect payment** (on the Bills page or the side bar).
2. Type the mobile or name and click **Find**. Or pick from **Biggest dues**.
3. Their unpaid bills show, oldest first, with the total due.
4. Untick any bill you want to skip.
5. Type **Amount received** (it is pre-filled with the full due).
6. Pick **Paid by**: Cash, UPI or Card. For UPI the QR shows with the amount.
7. Click the save button. A receipt can go on WhatsApp.

**If something goes wrong**
- *"That is more than the due":* fix the amount.
- *Wrong amount saved:* tell the Owner or Admin straight away. Do not collect again.

---

## 8. Car in, job card, technician, ready, bill

**When:** a customer leaves a car for work (fitting, PPF, coating and so on).

**Steps**
1. Click **Job cards**, then **Car in**.
2. **1, Customer and car:** type the mobile, name, car model and number plate.
3. **2, Work:** search the products or services, or type the work and press Enter. Set the price.
4. **3, Promised time:** pick a quick time or choose one. Pick the **Technician** or leave **Assign later**. Under **More details** you can add km, fuel, bay and notes (for example "Scratch on rear bumper").
5. Press **Ctrl+Enter** to save. On the next screen **Upload** the before photos.
6. Click **Print job sheet** if you want paper.
7. The job moves along the board: **Waiting, Working, QC, Ready, Delivered**. On a job, use the big button (**Start work**, **Ready for QC**, **Passed QC · Ready for pickup**, **Hand over · Delivered**). On the PC board you can also drag a card.
8. When it is **Ready**, click **Send ready on WhatsApp** so the customer knows.
9. When the customer collects, click **Make bill**. The work items go into a bill. Take payment and save as in section 2.

**If something goes wrong**
- *Car brought in by mistake:* click **Cancel job…** and type why.
- *Need to add work:* open the job and add it under **Work**.
- *Make bill is greyed or missing:* only some roles can bill. Ask the counter or Admin.

## 9. Technician: using the phone

**When:** you work on a car.

**Steps**
1. Sign in on your phone. You see **My jobs**: only the cars given to you. You see no money.
2. Open your car. You see the model, plate and the work list.
3. Tap **Start** when you begin.
4. Tick each work item when it is done.
5. Tap **Add photo** (or **Upload photos**) to take before and after photos.
6. When all work is done, tap **Ready for QC**. The counter checks it.

**If something goes wrong**
- *"No cars assigned to you right now":* ask the counter to assign a job from the job board. Then tap **Refresh**.
- *Photo did not go:* try again. Check the phone's internet.

---

## 10. Cash close at day end

**When:** every evening, after the last bill. Owner and Finance do this.

**Steps**
1. Click **Finance**, then **Cash close**.
2. The app shows the **Expected cash**: opening cash, plus cash received, minus cash expenses.
3. Count the cash in the drawer. Type the number in **Cash you counted in the drawer**.
4. If it is different, type a **Note** (for example "₹200 given to Sunil for fuel, not entered").
5. Click **Save cash close**.
6. Click **Send to owner on WhatsApp** to send the summary.

**If something goes wrong**
- *Difference is big:* count again. Check that every expense was entered (section 11).
- *Made a mistake after saving:* click **Redo close**.
- *Forgot yesterday:* the Owner sees "Cash close not done" on the Home page. Do it first thing.

---

## 11. Expenses

**When:** money leaves the shop (tea, fuel, rent, electricity, salaries).

**Steps**
1. Click **Finance**, then **Add expense** (or **Expenses**, then **Add expense**).
2. Type the **Amount**.
3. Pick **Spent on** (Rent, Electricity, Salaries, Tea & snacks, Transport, Supplies, Marketing, Repairs, Other).
4. Pick **Paid by** (Cash, UPI and so on). **Cash expenses come out of the drawer.**
5. Add **Paid to**, a note and a receipt photo if you have one.
6. Save.

**If something goes wrong:** open the expense and click **Edit**. If it was entered by mistake, click **Cancel expense**. It stays in history but stops counting.

---

## 12. Cancelling or returning a bill (Owner and Admin only)

**When:** a bill was wrong, or the customer returns the goods.

Sales staff do not see these buttons. Ask the Owner or Admin.

**Cancel the whole bill**
1. Open the bill (Bills, then the bill).
2. Open the **⋯** menu and click **Cancel bill**.
3. Pick the reason: **Wrong item, Customer returned, Duplicate, Other**. For Other, type details.
4. Click **Cancel bill**. (**Keep bill** goes back.)
5. The bill stays with a red **CANCELLED** stamp. Stock goes back. Money paid is recorded as refunded. The Owner sees it on Home.

**Return some items**
1. Open the bill and click **Return items**.
2. Choose the items and quantity coming back and type the **Reason**.
3. Click **Save credit note**. Stock goes back. The amount clears any due first, then the rest is refunded.

**If something goes wrong:** a cancelled bill can't be un-cancelled. Make a new bill.
